# Clock

Shows the time in the status bar. It updates as each minute starts, and
hovering over it shows the full date.

## How to use

1. Install the plugin. The time appears in the status bar, in your
   system's usual style (`14:05` or `2:05 PM`).
2. Hover over it to see today's date, such as *Sunday, 11 October 2026*.
3. Click it to switch between 24-hour and 12-hour time. Sable remembers
   your choice.

You can also switch from the command palette: ⇧⌘P (Ctrl+Shift+P on
Windows/Linux), then **Clock: Toggle 12/24-Hour Time**.

Permissions: none. Needs Sable 0.3.6 or newer.
